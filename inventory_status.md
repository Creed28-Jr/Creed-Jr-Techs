# Inventory Availability Knowledge

Use this document as the source for customer-facing answers about unavailable products.

## Canonical Out-of-Stock Response

When a product is explicitly marked out of stock in verified inventory data, say:

> Sorry, [product] is out of stock. I can help you find a similar item we have available.

If verified alternatives are available, name only those catalog items and ask whether the customer would like their prices. Do not show or recommend an item that is also marked out of stock.

## Product Not Listed

If a requested product is not in the catalog, say:

> Sorry, we don't currently stock [product]. I can help you find a similar product we do carry.

Suggest alternatives only from the known product catalog. If no relevant alternative is known, invite the customer to browse phones, accessories, TVs, speakers, or laptops without presenting unrelated items as substitutes.

## Availability Rules

- A product being listed in the catalog does not prove that it is currently in stock.
- Do not infer stock status from a product's condition, price, brand, or presence in the catalog.
- Say a specific product is out of stock only when verified inventory data explicitly marks it that way.
- Never invent availability, quantities, restock dates, prices, or compatible alternatives.
- When inventory information is missing or stale, say that availability needs to be checked with shop staff.
